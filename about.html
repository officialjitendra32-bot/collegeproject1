ABOUT- Campus-BNCET

Campus – BNCET is a renowned institution well known for its quality
education. It has a lush-green campus spreading over an area of 10 acres. It is
situated on NH-24, Bakshi ka Talab, Lucknow. The Institute is equipped with
all modern amenities. The atmosphere of the campus not only provides a
quality learning ambiance but also creates an opportunity to develop
entrepreneurship linkages with the corporate world.



ABOUT- CONFERENCE HALL

Conference room- A conference room with all modern facilities with the seating capacity of 120 students. Conference rooms are the best environments for making decisions and having important discussions. A traditional conference room is an important space for the growth of an organization.



ABOUT- LIBRARY


LIBRARY hall- The institute has well equipped LIBRARY hall. It serves the
purpose of training and presentations.
